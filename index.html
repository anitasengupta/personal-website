Anita Sengupta is a designer, artist, and writer based in Berlin.

She has collaborated with artists and institutions across the arts, culture, and technology sectors, including Marina Abramovic / CIRCA, Ian Cheng, the Tezos Foundation, Anava Projects, and Joy Space.

Her writing has appeared in a handful of publications, including PANTA and Ladybeard. She has received support from the Community of Writers and the Kenyon Review Writing Workshop.
